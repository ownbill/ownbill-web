How a deal works

One requirement. Five verified quotes. One bill.

  1. 01

    Ask for a price, like you'd ask a friend.

    MandiWay asks the right questions for the product: variety, pack, quantity, by when, your budget. Type or speak. Choose whether sellers may call you.

  2. 02

    Up to five verified sellers see it.

    Sellers who deal in it nearby get the requirement with your specs and budget. Each must reply within 24 hours or give the slot back.

  3. 03

    Check who you're paying.

    Every seller's trust card shows their checks, reply speed, ratings and delivered orders. Type any GSTIN or mobile number to verify before you pay.

  4. 04

    Agree, order, and the bill makes itself.

    Accept a quote and it becomes an order. Sellers on OwnBill get a GST bill on both sides automatically, and their stock moves with it.

Questions traders ask.

When does MandiWay launch?

Soon, on Android first, as two apps: MandiWay for buyers and MandiWay Seller. Join the early list and we'll tell you the day it opens.

How is this different from the lead directories?

A requirement goes to five verified sellers at most, and each must reply within 24 hours or give the slot to the next seller. Buyers choose chat or calls. Every seller carries a trust card built from real checks and real orders, not from what they paid.

What does "verified" mean?

MandiWay checks four things: the GSTIN is active, the bank account is in the business's name, the owner's identity, and the premises on a live video call. You can see which checks a seller has passed.

Is it free for buyers?

Yes. Searching, asking for prices, chatting and verifying a seller are free for buyers.

How do auctions and escrow work?

Buyers place a refundable deposit to bid. The winner's payment is held in escrow and released to the seller only after the goods are accepted.

Do I need OwnBill to sell on MandiWay?

No. You can list products in MandiWay Seller directly. If you use OwnBill, linking brings your items, prices and stock across, and orders become bills in OwnBill automatically.

Where are the rates from?

MandiWay's rates desk publishes daily rates at launch. Until then, the app shows sample rates and says so.

What is MandiWay Global?

An import desk for bulk buyers, starting soon after launch: verified overseas suppliers, inspection-backed quotes, and payment straight from your bank to the supplier.
