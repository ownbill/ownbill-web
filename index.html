The right GST on every bill, without looking it up.

Add your GSTIN once. OwnBill reads your customer's GSTIN and picks CGST and SGST, IGST or UTGST for you, and prints everything a tax invoice must carry.

Every kind of bill
Tax invoice, bill of supply, estimate, sale order, proforma, delivery challan, credit note.
Exports
Foreign currency, LUT or IGST paid, shipping bill details you can add after saving.
E-way bill and e-invoice
OwnBill makes the official upload file for each bill, and prints the IRN and QR once you have them.
Ready for your CA
GSTR-1 file, Tally export, an Excel with every bill, and TDS and TCS kept straight.

Know who owes you, and get it back sooner.

Every customer has a khata with a running balance. OwnBill tells you who is late and by how many days, and makes the nudge one tap.

UPI on every bill
A pay link and QR code with the amount already filled in, on the bill, the receipt and the statement.
Reminders on WhatsApp
A short list each morning of who to remind, with the message and pay link ready to send.
Promises remembered
Note when a customer says they will pay. Reminders wait until that day.
A statement they can open
Share a link that shows the customer their own bills and balance, and switch it off any time.

Stock that adds up, in every godown.

Every bill and purchase moves stock for you. See what is running low before a customer asks for it.

Godowns and transfers
Stock per shop or godown, and a numbered transfer when goods move between them.
Batches and expiry
Sells the batch that expires first, never an expired one, and lists what expires in 30, 60 or 90 days.
Serial and IMEI numbers
Scan them in on purchase and out on sale. Look one up to see when it was bought and sold.
Low stock, before it runs out
Items running low show on Home, and the reorder list goes to your supplier on WhatsApp.

One shop, many phones, nothing lost.

Your cashier bills at the counter, your manager checks stock in the godown, and you see today's sales from home. Each person sees only what you allow.

Works without internet
Keep billing when the network drops. Everything syncs when it is back.
Staff with their own login
Cashier, manager or read-only access for your CA. Attendance and salary on the Business plan.
Deleted by mistake?
Deleted bills stay in the trash for 30 days, with their payments and stock, ready to bring back.
On your computer too
A Windows app for the counter, with keyboard shortcuts for a new bill and quick search.
