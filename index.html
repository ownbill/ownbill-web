Everything your shop runs on. In one app.

The right GST on every bill, without looking it up.

  • Every kind of bill. Tax invoice, bill of supply, estimate, proforma, sale order, delivery challan, credit and debit notes.
  • CGST, SGST, IGST or UTGST chosen from the two GSTINs, with HSN or SAC on every line.
  • E-invoice ready. The official JSON for each bill, and the IRN, acknowledgement and QR printed once you have them.
  • E-way bill file with transport details, ready to upload on the portal.
  • Exports. Foreign currency, LUT or IGST paid, shipping bill number, date and port.
  • TDS and TCS on bills and party payments, and credit notes that quote the original invoice.

Know who owes you. Get it back sooner.

  • A khata for every party with a running balance, “you'll get” and “you'll give”, and how many days late.
  • Reminders on WhatsApp. A daily list of who to remind, with a polite message and pay link ready.
  • Promises remembered. Note when they say they'll pay; reminders wait till that day.
  • A statement link your customer can open to see their own bills and balance. Switch it off any time.
  • UPI on every bill, receipt and statement, with the amount filled in. Several UPI and bank accounts.
  • Special prices for a customer, applied on their bills automatically.

Stock that adds up, in every godown.

  • Moves by itself with every bill, purchase and return. Stock value always on screen.

  • Purchase bills with supplier GST, so input tax shows in your GST summary.
  • Low stock on Home, and a reorder list you send to your supplier on WhatsApp.

Questions shop owners ask.

Does OwnBill work without internet?

Yes. Billing, khata and stock work fully offline on the phone. When the network is back, every phone in your shop syncs on its own.

Is it GST compliant?

OwnBill picks CGST and SGST, IGST or UTGST from the two GSTINs and prints what a tax invoice must carry, including HSN, place of supply, the reverse-charge line and the amount in words. It makes the GSTR-1 file, e-invoice JSON and e-way bill file. Rates and HSN codes are defaults you can change; check them with your CA.

What happens after the 6 free months?

You choose Starter, Business or Max. If you don't, the paid features stop, but your bills stay readable on your phone and can be exported.

Can my staff use it on their own phones?

Yes. Invite them by number and give each a role: manager, cashier or read-only for your CA. Big discounts and deletes need your PIN.

How do my customers pay?

With any UPI app. Every bill, receipt and statement carries a QR and a pay link with the amount filled in. The money goes straight to your own UPI or bank account; OwnBill never touches it.

Is my data safe?

A copy is kept on servers in Mumbai, encrypted, with rules that keep every shop's data apart. You can also back up daily to your own Google Drive. We never sell your data or read your bills.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Spanish, Indonesian and Portuguese.

Does it suit pharmacies, mobile shops and wholesalers?

Yes. Batches and expiry for medicines, serial and IMEI numbers for electronics, and godowns, transfers and purchase bills for wholesale and distribution.

Is there a computer version?

Yes, a Windows app for the counter that stays in step with your phones.

When is MandiWay coming?

Soon. OwnBill Max users get access first. Write to us to hear the day it opens.
