Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Provantic Technologies Private Limited, Bengaluru, India.

In short: a copy of your business data is kept on servers in India so all your phones stay in step and nothing is lost. We never sell your data, show your bills to anyone you have not allowed, or read them ourselves.

1. Who is responsible

Provantic Technologies Private Limited runs OwnBill and decides how your account data is used. The details you keep about your own customers and suppliers belong to your business: we store them only to run OwnBill for you.

4. Messages to your customers

When you send a bill or a payment reminder, OwnBill opens WhatsApp on your phone with the message ready, and you send it yourself. Customers can ask you to stop, and you can switch reminders off for any customer.

5. Who can see your data

You, and the staff you invite, each limited by the role you give them (manager, cashier, CA). A customer statement link shows that one customer their own balance, bills and payments until you switch it off. Provantic's admin tools show your account, plan and phones, never your bills or customers, and every admin action is logged. We may disclose data if Indian law requires it.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Rules on the server keep every shop's data apart, so one shop can never read or change another's. Passwords for e-invoicing are kept in an encrypted vault that the app itself never reads back.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is open. If you delete your account, your cloud data and sign-in are erased 7 days later. Records we must keep by law, such as our own invoices to you, are kept for the period the law sets. GST law asks you to keep your invoices for at least 6 years, so keep a backup before deleting.

8. Your rights

You can see, correct and export your data in the app, withdraw consent, and delete your account at any time (how). You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

OwnBill is a business tool for people 18 and over.

10. Changes

We show important changes in the app before they apply.
